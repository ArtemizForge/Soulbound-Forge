# Soulbound Forge

A fan-made character creator for **Warhammer Age of Sigmar: Soulbound** (Cubicle 7).
<img src="https://storage.ko-fi.com/cdn/useruploads/e3aa5b3d-d477-4fdc-9a20-43eaa6ae71e1_png_c2b22b28-a2ee-4336-9394-f5489ca4e07fcover.png?v=13cf4a42-57e5-4c84-b5d1-b7cd374eb154"></img>
Build a character step by step: Species and Faction, archetype, attributes, skills, talents,
spells, equipment and finish on a printable character sheet.
Every talent, spell and miracle entry shows a **sourcebook and page number**, so the tool
refers you back to the books. You need the rulebooks to play, and honestly you should own them.
Cubicle 7 made an awesome game.

---

## Use it

### ▶ Play in your browser

**https://soulbound-forge.netlify.app/**

Works on phone, tablet and desktop. On a phone or tablet you can add it to your home screen
and it will keep working with no signal.

### ⬇ Or download the offline version

**https://github.com/ArtemizForge/Soulbound-Forge/releases/latest**

One single `.html` file. Download it, double-click it, and it runs completely offline — no
internet, no install, no server. Handy for a table with bad wifi, or for emailing to your
players so everyone has their own copy.

---

## Good to know

- **Your character stays on your device.** There's no account, no server, nothing is uploaded.
  Save a character to a `.json` file if you want to move it between devices or back it up.
- **The archetype emblems are AI-generated placeholders.** All 78 of them. They're there so the
  tool doesn't look empty, not because I think they're the right art for it. If you're an artist
  and would like to see your work in here instead, I would love that — get in touch and you'll
  be credited.
- **The tool never blocks your concept.** A pick that sits outside the printed list for your
  species or archetype stays selectable — it's flagged **ATYPICAL** rather than disabled, so you
  and your GM get to make the call.
